## How delivery works

```
Discover ──▶ Design ──▶ Deliver ──▶ Drive
```

1. **Discover** — Understand the business process, priorities and constraints.
2. **Design** — Agree the architecture, responsibilities and delivery approach.
3. **Deliver** — Implement and test against agreed acceptance criteria.
4. **Drive** — Operate or hand over with clear ownership and practical next steps.

**Governed, not just delivered.** Named responsibility. Monitored services. Prioritised changes. Clear escalation routes.
